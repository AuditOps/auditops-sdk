## Who should pay for an audit?

| Who Pays                 | Pros                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Cons                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Seller (Vendor) Pays** | - **Logical:** They want to sell their product, so they should pay for the audit demonstrating it is secure and trustworthy.<br>- **Scalability:** A single audit can be shared with hundreds or thousands of prospective customers.<br>- **Market Efficiency:** Eliminates the need for every customer to perform their own assessment.<br>- **Lower Cost for Buyers:** Reduces procurement friction and speeds up sales. | - **Conflict of Interest:** The audit firm is hired and paid by the organization being audited, creating a perceived or actual incentive to produce favorable results.<br>- **Audit Quality:** Without strong professional standards and oversight, audit quality can suffer and reports lose credibility.<br>- **Scope Shopping:** Vendors may seek firms willing to perform less rigorous assessments or limit audit scope.<br>- **Buyer Beware:** Buyers must evaluate not only the report, but also the reputation and quality of the audit firm. |
| **Buyer Pays**           | - **Alignment:** The buyer has the strongest incentive to ensure the audit adequately identifies and mitigates risk.<br>- **Independence:** The auditor's incentives are more closely aligned with the party relying on the report.<br>- **Tailored Assurance:** Audits can focus on the specific risks and concerns of the buyer.                                                                                         | - **Audit Burden:** Multiple customers often commission nearly identical audits, creating significant duplication of effort for vendors.<br>- **Higher Cost:** The total cost across the market increases as the same work is repeated.<br>- **Sales Friction:** Vendors must repeatedly respond to questionnaires, evidence requests, and on-site assessments.<br>- **Inconsistent Requirements:** Different buyers request different evidence and controls, making standardization difficult.                                                       |
