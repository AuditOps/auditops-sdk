# How to Share an AuditOps Report

Send your prospective customer an email similar to the example below. Sit back, grab a coffee, and wait for them to sign the contract.

### Email Template

**To:** `Prospective Buyer`  
**From:** `Sales Rep`  
**Subject:** AuditOps Report & Trust Center

```text
Hey [Buyer Contact Name],

I hope you’re doing well! 

Based on our last conversation, I wanted to share some more information about our security posture! Here are some links that should help answer questions about how we protect your data:
* Trust Center: LINK TO TRUST CENTER
* Security FAQ's: LINK TO SECURITY FAQ's

I'm also attaching our most recent AuditOps report. This is report demonstrates our compliance with AWS best practices, without sharing any sensitive data. You can learn more about the project here: https://github.com/AuditOps/auditops-sdk

We're really excited to partner together! Please let me know if you have any other questions or if you need anything else from us!

Best,
[Your Name]
```
